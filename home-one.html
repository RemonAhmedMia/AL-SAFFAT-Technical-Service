AL SAFFAT FACILITIES MANAGEMENT

In 1974, Al SAFFAT Facilities Management (FM) began its journey as a mechanical contractor specialising in the installation and maintenance of the mechanical system. Today, some 47 years after our founding, we are the largest full-service facilities management company in the United Arab Emirates. Services include Integrated Facility Management, Energy Consultancy & Management, Security & Smart Solutions, Hospitality Services, Engineering & Maintenance of Equipment, and a comprehensive range of cleaning services, driven by a team of devoted workers that work closely with the customers. An Energy Services Company accreditation from RSB is something the company takes great pride in.

In the fields of infrastructure, luxury real estate, industrial facilities, mixed-use developments, commercial and residential properties, as well as retail and educational institutions, we are the industry leaders in providing FM services. A firm affiliated with the Al Shirawi Group, the UAE's largest commercial conglomerate, allows us to draw on a plethora of experience from across the board.
